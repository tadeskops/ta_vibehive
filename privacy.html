Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

VibeHive is a community event and contribution management app operated by The Address Co-operative Housing Society Ltd., Baner, Pune (registration no. PNA/PNA(2)/HSG/(TC)/29730/2025-2026), for the sole benefit of its residents and members. This policy explains what data we collect when you sign in, and what we do (and do not) do with it.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to anyone who signs in to VibeHive at https://tadeskops.github.io/ta_vibehive/ using a Google, Microsoft, or other supported identity provider.

2. What data we receive when you sign in

When you tap "Continue with Google" (or another provider), the provider returns only these fields to us, and nothing else:

  • Email address — used as your unique identifier inside the app so your contributions are attributed to you.
  • Display name — shown next to your contributions and on your profile; you can edit it afterwards.
  • Stable user ID (called sub) — a random string the provider assigns you. Used for account matching only.

We do not receive or request: your phone number, your home address, your contacts, your calendar, your emails, your photos, your Google Drive files, or any offline access token. The scopes requested are strictly the OpenID Connect minimum: openid email profile.

3. Where your data lives

VibeHive is a static single-page web app hosted on GitHub Pages. Your identity data is stored in your browser's local storage on the device you signed in from. There is no VibeHive server-side database and no VibeHive backend that can read or aggregate resident data.

Committee-managed data (event definitions, contribution ledgers, receipts) is stored in files inside the app repository. Personally identifying fields (name, flat number, email) inside those files are visible to anyone with a committee role and to the software supply chain of the repository host (GitHub Inc.).

4. What we do with your data

  • Attribute contributions — link the payment you record to your identity so the committee can reconcile receipts.
  • Show contributor lists — display your name against events you contributed to (you may opt into anonymous mode per event).
  • Notify you — surface event reminders and receipt confirmations inside the app.

We do not sell, rent, share, or advertise against your data. There is no analytics tracker, no cookie banner, and no third-party marketing pixel embedded in the site.

5. What the identity provider knows

Google, Microsoft, and any other identity provider you sign in with will know that you used them to access VibeHive. They log that access under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement

6. Deleting your data

To remove your account and everything we hold about you:

  1. Sign in, open the whoami menu, tap Sign out. This clears your session on the device.
  2. Email ta.deskops@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my VibeHive account" from your registered email address. We will remove your user record from the committee-managed files within 14 days and confirm by reply.
  3. Optionally, revoke VibeHive's access at your identity provider: Google account permissions.

7. Access, correction, and portability

You can view, edit, and export the data associated with your account from inside the app: whoami menu → Profile. All fields are editable except your email (which is the account key). To export a copy of your contribution history, use the Export action on your profile page (produces a JSON file).

8. Children

VibeHive is intended for adult residents of the society. If a resident under the age of 18 signs in, their parent or legal guardian may request account deletion using §6.

9. Security

Sign-in uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE (RFC 7636). No password is transmitted to or stored by VibeHive. Data in transit uses HTTPS end-to-end. Because the app is client-side and open source, its full source is publicly auditable at github.com/tadeskops/ta_vibehive.

10. Changes to this policy

If we ever materially change what data we collect or what we do with it, we will update this page and post a banner in the app for at least 30 days before the change takes effect.

11. Contact

Questions, corrections, or data-deletion requests: ta.deskops@gmail.com.
